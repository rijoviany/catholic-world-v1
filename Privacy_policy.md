1. Introduction

Welcome to Catholic World ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you download, install, and use Catholic World.

By using Catholic World, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect
A. Location Data
Types of Location Collected: Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) and Precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION).
Purpose: To provide location-based Catholic services, such as:
Locating nearby Catholic churches, parishes, and shrines.
Providing accurate local Mass schedules, confession times, and parish events.
Calculating localized liturgical calendars, feast days, or prayer times.
Handling: Location data is accessed only when permission is granted by you in the app. Location data is processed in real time and is not sold, rented, or used for behavioral advertising.
B. Push Notifications & Device Identifiers
Permissions: POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and INTERNET.
Data Collected: Unique Device Token (FCM Registration Token), device model, operating system version.
Purpose: We utilize Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver optional push notifications, such as:
Daily gospel reflections, daily saint reminders, and prayer alerts.
Breaking news or urgent community prayer intentions.
Control: You can enable or disable push notifications at any time in your device's System Settings under Settings > Apps > Catholic World > Notifications.
C. Automatically Collected Usage & Diagnostic Data
Device & Connection Data: IP address, network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), basic device hardware details.
Crash & Performance Data: Crash logs and performance diagnostics to fix bugs, optimize reliability, and improve user experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

Delivering core app features (scripture readings, prayers, mass locator, audio/media).
Personalizing nearby parish search results based on your current location.
Sending requested notifications and liturgical alerts.
Detecting, investigating, and preventing technical errors or app crashes.
5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. We will only disclose your information in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure providers (such as Google Firebase) solely to operate the app's essential features.
Legal Compliance: When required by applicable law, court subpoena, or valid governmental order.
Safety & Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the developer, or the general public.
6. Data Retention and Security
Retention: We retain anonymous technical tokens (e.g., FCM push tokens) only for as long as necessary to deliver notifications or until you uninstall the app / revoke notification permissions. Location queries are processed on-demand and not stored persistently on external servers.
Security: We implement standard security practices and TLS/HTTPS encrypted transmissions to protect data during network flights. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA in California, or LGPD in Brazil), you have rights regarding your personal information:

8. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Catholic World is designed for general audiences and families. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action to delete such data.
